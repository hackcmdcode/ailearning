How ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini Actually Work

👤 AI Academy Editorial Team 📅 Updated July 2026 ⏱ 13 min read

Frontier AI systems such as OpenAI's ChatGPT, Anthropic's Claude, and Google's Gemini represent the cutting edge of conversational intelligence. While all three are based on transformer neural network architectures, each company has developed distinct technical methodologies for training, safety alignment, multimodal reasoning, and internal reasoning chains.

To grasp the foundational math behind these models, read What is a Large Language Model (LLM)?.

OpenAI ChatGPT & Reasoning Models

ChatGPT is powered by OpenAI's GPT family and advanced reasoning models (such as o1 and o3). Key innovations include:

  • Reasoning Tokens (Chain of Thought): Advanced models generate hidden reasoning tokens before answering complex math or coding problems, evaluating multiple candidate steps before finalizing their output.
  • RLHF Alignment: Fine-tuned with human evaluators using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) to maintain helpfulness and guardrails.

Anthropic Claude & Constitutional AI

Anthropic's Claude family (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.7) is renowned for coding precision, nuanced writing, and exceptionally large context windows (up to 200k+ tokens). Key architectural highlights include:

💡 Constitutional AI (RLAIF)

Rather than relying solely on human review, Anthropic uses Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback (RLAIF) guided by a set of written principles (a "Constitution") to automatically critique and align responses against ethical standards.

Google Gemini & Native Multimodality

Google's Gemini was architected from the ground up as a natively multimodal model. Unlike earlier systems that retrofitted separate computer vision models, Gemini ingests text, audio, video frames, and code simultaneously in a single unified transformer encoder. Discover visual processing in Computer Vision Basics.
